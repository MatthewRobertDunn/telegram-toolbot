# Reflection Prompts

Use selectively. These are prompts, not a questionnaire. Ask one at a time and follow the person's language.

## Understanding a moment
- What was happening just before this started?
- What mattered to you in that situation?
- What did you notice in your thoughts, feelings, body, and urges?
- What did you do next?

## Understanding behaviour
- What did that response help with in the short term?
- What happened later?
- Does this response move you towards or away from something you care about?
- Is there a safety or practical reason for responding this way?

## Defusion
- What is your mind telling you right now?
- Can you notice that as a thought rather than a command?
- What does the thought urge you to do?
- Does it need practical attention, or can it be present while you choose?

## Acceptance and willingness
- What feeling are you trying hardest not to have?
- What happens when you struggle with it?
- Would making a little room for it help you take a chosen step?
- Is there something in the external situation that needs to change?

## Present moment
- What is happening around you right now?
- Where would it be useful to place your attention?
- What is one thing you can notice without needing to change it?

## Values
- What matters to you about this?
- How would you like to show up?
- Which part feels chosen by you?
- What quality of action would you like to practise?

## Committed action
- What is the smallest meaningful step?
- What might get in the way?
- What could you do if that barrier appears?
- Is this action realistic and kind to your actual limits?

## Review
- What did you learn?
- What helped, even a little?
- What would you change next time?
- Is there anything you would rather leave alone for now?
